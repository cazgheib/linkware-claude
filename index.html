What we do

Specialist services for complex capital markets projects

From initial scoping through to post-go-live stabilisation — Linkware provides hands-on consulting across the full implementation lifecycle, with deep expertise in derivatives, fixed income, and commodities.

Implementation consulting

End-to-end programme management and delivery consulting for trading and risk system implementations.

  • Programme governance and delivery framework design
  • Requirements gathering and functional specification
  • Stakeholder management across front office, IT, and operations
  • Milestone tracking, risk identification, and escalation management
  • Cutover planning and go-live execution

System configuration & setup

Hands-on configuration of trading platforms and risk systems across rates, FX, credit, fixed income, and commodities.

  • Instrument and product setup across all asset classes
  • Trade booking model design and configuration
  • Reference data architecture and static data management
  • Workflow and lifecycle configuration
  • Interface design and integration support

UAT management

Rigorous, structured UAT programmes that go beyond surface testing, covering edge cases and exception flows.

  • UAT strategy design and test plan development
  • Test script authoring covering full trade lifecycle
  • Defect management and root cause triage
  • Business tester coordination and support
  • Sign-off facilitation and formal UAT closure

Project rescue & recovery

When a programme has lost direction — Linkware steps in with an objective assessment and a clear recovery plan.

  • Independent programme health assessment
  • Root cause analysis of delivery failures
  • Recovery plan design and stakeholder re-engagement
  • Interim programme management through stabilisation
  • Post-recovery handover and knowledge transfer

Training & workshops

Practical, outcome-oriented training designed for capital markets teams. Whether you need to upskill a project team before go-live, onboard operations staff, or run a discovery workshop with senior stakeholders.

  • System training for front office, operations, and IT teams
  • Discovery and requirements workshops
  • Trade lifecycle and booking model workshops
  • Post-go-live knowledge transfer sessions

About Linkware

Built on a decade of real delivery

Linkware is a capital markets technology consultancy founded on a simple premise: that the best implementation advice comes from people who have actually done the work — not from slide decks and methodology frameworks.

With over ten years of hands-on experience delivering trading and risk system programmes for banks, asset managers, and fintech firms, we bring practical expertise to the full implementation lifecycle. Our work spans derivatives across rates, FX, and credit, fixed income, and commodities.

How we think

Three principles that shape every Linkware engagement — from initial scoping call to final handover.

Honesty before comfort
We tell clients what they need to hear, not what they want to hear. If a timeline is unrealistic, a scope is under-defined, or a go-live decision is premature — we say so. Clearly, and early.
Expertise over process
Methodology frameworks have their place. But no framework substitutes for someone who has been in the room when a migration goes wrong at midnight before go-live. Experience is the product.
Outcomes over outputs
Documents, decks, and deliverables are means to an end. The end is a system in production, working as intended, used with confidence by the people it was built for. That is the only measure that matters.
